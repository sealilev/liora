In 1996, I started my journey in the world of hi-tech as a software developer. Since then, I have advanced through management positions - starting with project management, through testing and quality assurance (QA), a field in which I became an expert.

After about 4 years as a Product Manager, I became independent. As a freelancer, I consulted for companies in the QA field, and taught testing and automation for about 15 years at leading colleges in the market.

Today, I build focused solutions in various fields based on client needs. I don't build "grandiose" software - I specialize in personal and practical solutions for specific content areas.
